Don't quit for an extended period of time

When you start programming for the first time, make sure that you don't quit for an extended period of time.

If you encounter a roadblock or feelings of inability, just push through and do something every day, even if it is small. Every action in succession builds the overall skills and stopping for any more than a few days can make the process even more difficult.
