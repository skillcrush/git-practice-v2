Hi, my name is Rithwik and I have some advice for you!

Getting comfortable with being uncomfortable!

The fear and anxiety you feel in learning something new especially when you are not in the school stage of life is tough. There's a lot of expectations riding on you from others and from yourself. We do a lot of weighing for everything. What if I can't learn well, what will happen? What if this doesn't work? What am I doing with my life. There can be lot's of self doubt. You might feel these and many more things. But don't worry and keep going.

This is part of the process. Just know that even through all this you ARE moving forward. You know more today than yesterday. And this will accumulate over time. Just know that it is ok to feel this discomfort. You will still make progress despite such states of mind. So get comfortable with feeling uncomfortable. We are all here for you!

Dedicated Time

When things get overwhelming, a small step like dedicating a particular time period everyday will help. Even if the time period is small like few minutes. Because once you start doing things, trust me, things will slowly (yes slowly) fall into place. The important thing is showing up everyday and YOU WILL MAKE PROGRESS and YOU WILL FEEL BETTER THAN YESTERDAY.

So decide on a time period everyday and give it your all

Overcoming overthinking to get going

When there's a lot of thoughts making you anxious, just take a small step. ANY SMALL STEP WILL DO

These are things I generally do

  1. First, I open the computer and login
  2. Next step, I open the skillcrush portal. I am not thinking of anything. Just opening the portal nothing else
  3. Whew! Once this is done, I open a class or lesson. Not learning just opening a link and nothing more
  4. Alright! Then just read a few lines. No stress about learning. It's just reading a few lines
  5. Hmmm! I don't understand this. That's ok. Let me use some resources available or ask someone. No stress!! just another step

And keep going like this :)

Learning is a step by step process

Another thing to know is that learning is a step by step and slow process. Ever seen a big, tall and huge skyscraper being built? It takes time. The foundation takes time and is the first step. That's what you are building now. Different buildings take different amounts of time and different parts of the building take differing amounts of time.

Remember, it takes the time it takes. Just make sure that you are sincere and that you are really putting in the best effort that you can under the given circumstances. YES, UNDER THE GIVEN CIRCUMSTANCES.

Being as healthy as you can!

Do look after yourself as best as you can. Get proper rest and food.... again UNDER YOUR GIVEN CIRCUMSTANCES. Don't worry about what you cannot do or what is not possible.
