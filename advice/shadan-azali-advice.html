Hi, my name is Shadan and I have some advice for you!

Don't wait until you feel "ready."

Build the small thing, push it to GitHub, and fix it in your next commit. Progress beats perfection, and every project you ship teaches you something a tutorial can't.

Commit often, and write messages your future self will understand.

A clear commit history is like a trail of breadcrumbs. When something breaks (and it will!), you'll be so glad you can see exactly what changed and when.

Stuck for more than 20 minutes? Ask for help.

The Skillcrush community is one of the best resources in this program. Asking questions isn't a sign you're behind; it's how developers work every single day.
